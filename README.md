# 🏛️ The Lost Bukhara

> A historical stealth adventure game created in **48 hours** for a Game Jam.

---

## 🎮 About the Game

**The Lost Bukhara** is a short historical adventure inspired by the Mongol invasion of Bukhara.

During the invasion, countless scientific manuscripts were at risk of being destroyed. Your mission is to secretly recover these valuable manuscripts and escape the city before they are lost forever.

The game focuses on preserving knowledge rather than combat, delivering a simple but meaningful gameplay experience.

---

## 📖 Story

The Mongol army has occupied the city of **Bukhara**.

Ancient libraries are being destroyed, and priceless scientific manuscripts are disappearing.

You play as a brave citizen whose goal is to rescue these manuscripts and safely smuggle them out of the city before it's too late.

Can you save the knowledge of an entire civilization?

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏛️ Historical setting inspired by the Mongol invasion of Bukhara
- 📜 Collect hidden scientific manuscripts
- 🥷 Simple stealth-style gameplay
- ⏱️ Approximately 5 minutes of gameplay
- 🎮 Built in only **48 hours** during a Game Jam
- 💻 Developed with Unity

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay

Your objective is simple:

1. Explore the city.
2. Find hidden scientific manuscripts.
3. Avoid danger.
4. Escape the city with as many manuscripts as possible.
